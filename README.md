# 🛍️ Customer Data Analysis | SQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior and sales performance using a retail shopping dataset containing **99,457 records** from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul, covering the period from 2021 to 2023.

The main objective is to understand customer purchasing patterns, identify sales trends, analyze product category preferences, and discover how factors such as gender, age, payment methods, and shopping malls influence customer purchases.

Using **MySQL and Power BI**, I performed data analysis and developed an interactive dashboard to transform raw shopping data into meaningful business insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer shopping distribution based on gender.
- Understand purchasing behavior across different age groups.
- Identify popular product categories and their revenue contribution.
- Compare revenue generated through different payment methods.
- Analyze sales performance across shopping malls.
- Identify monthly revenue trends.
- Present key business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains customer shopping transactions from 10 shopping malls in Istanbul.

**Dataset size:** 99,457 rows

**Time period:** 2021–2023

### Key Columns

- `invoice_no` – Unique invoice number
- `customer_id` – Customer identification
- `gender` – Customer gender
- `age` – Customer age
- `category` – Product category
- `quantity` – Number of products purchased
- `price` – Product price in Turkish Lira (TL)
- `payment_method` – Payment method used
- `invoice_date` – Date of purchase
- `shopping_mall` – Shopping mall name
